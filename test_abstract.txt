TITLE:
Pruning-Resistant Content Persistence on BlockDAG Ledgers

ABSTRACT:
We present a protocol for persistent content discovery on high-throughput BlockDAG ledgers with aggressive pruning. Signed records are replayable by third parties, turning persistence into an incentivized market rather than a protocol obligation. Evidence of pruned transactions remains verifiable through SPV bundles anchored to proof-of-work headers. We demonstrate the design on Kaspa testnet with storefront, application, and academic publishing rails sharing one record format.
